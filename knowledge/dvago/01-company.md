# About DVAGO

Status: public — dvago.pk, App Store and Google Play listings, verified 10 August 2026.

DVAGO is a Pakistani pharmacy and healthcare retailer. It sells online through
its app and website and through physical stores, and describes itself as
"Pharmacy & Wellness Experts".

- Over 10,000 authentic and temperature-controlled medicines and personal care
  products, across more than 400 brands.
- Customer helpline: 021-11-11-38246.
- Apps on iOS and Android; orders also taken on the website.

## What DVAGO sells

- **Medicines** — prescription and over-the-counter, across dermatology,
  gastroenterology, cardiology, neurology, respiratory, diabetes and more.
- **Baby & Mother Care** — diapers, infant formula, baby food, nursing aids.
- **Nutrition & Supplements** — multivitamins, calcium, iron, adult and child
  nutrition powders.
- **Personal Care** — skin, hair, oral and feminine care.
- **Devices & Support** — BP monitors, glucometers and strips, nebulisers,
  thermometers, oximeters, mobility aids.
- **Beauty Care** — cosmetics and skincare.
- **Foods & Beverages** — juices, tea, breakfast items and snacks.

## Where there are stores

Retail stores operate in **Karachi, Lahore, Hyderabad, Multan, Bahawalpur,
Kasur, Sahiwal and Burewala**. Some branches are open 24 hours.

Everywhere else in Pakistan is served by courier, not by a store. This
distinction decides what the agent is allowed to promise: one-hour delivery is
only possible from a branch that supports it. A caller in Peshawar or Quetta
gets courier delivery in two to three working days.

## Talk to a pharmacist

DVAGO offers a "Talk to our Pharmacist" service for questions about how to
take a medicine, dosage, and interactions. Pharmacists are available
**9am to 6pm**.

Anything clinical goes to a pharmacist. The agent may read out what is on the
label — strength, pack size, storage, whether a prescription is needed — and
must not advise on dosage, suitability, interactions or whether one medicine
can replace another.
